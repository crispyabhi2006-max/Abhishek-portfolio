ASSETS DIRECTORY FOR ABHISHEK S PORTFOLIO
==========================================

1. PROFILE PHOTO:
   Location: frontend/assets/profile.jpg
   Or: public/frontend/assets/profile.jpg
   
   To use your real photo:
   Save your headshot/photo as "profile.jpg" in this folder.
   The hero section will automatically display your real photograph.
   If no photo is present yet, an initials & developer avatar is shown automatically.

2. RESUME PDF:
   Location: frontend/assets/Abhishek_S_Resume.pdf
   Or: public/frontend/assets/Abhishek_S_Resume.pdf
   
   To update your resume:
   Export your updated resume from Word/Canva/LaTeX as "Abhishek_S_Resume.pdf"
   and place it in this folder. The "DOWNLOAD RESUME" button will download it immediately.
